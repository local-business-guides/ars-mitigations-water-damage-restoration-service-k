How the restoration work can proceed

The first step is to discuss the damage and arrange an assessment. The restoration team can identify affected areas and explain what should happen next. Depending on the situation, work may include removing water, drying wet materials, cleaning affected areas, and planning repairs. If mold is a concern, ask how remediation fits into the work.

A few terms may come up during the process. Mitigation means steps taken to limit further damage. Remediation refers to addressing a problem such as mold. Restoration means work to return affected areas to usable condition. Ask the provider to explain any term you do not recognize, along with what it means for your home or business.
