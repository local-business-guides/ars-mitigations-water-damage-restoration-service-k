Work, timing, costs, and terms to know

Water restoration can involve cleanup and attention to affected areas; the specific scope depends on the damage. Use these key facts as a starting point when you speak with a provider:

  • What it covers: Ask which affected areas and materials are included, and whether related cleanup is part of the proposed work.
  • Typical timing: The duration varies with the extent of the damage, the materials involved, access to affected areas, and drying needs. Ask what can be assessed now and what could change the schedule.
  • Cost factors: The size and location of the affected area, the materials involved, and the amount of work required can affect the overall cost. Ask for the scope and cost to be explained before work begins.
  • Terms you may hear: “Mitigation” means limiting further damage. “Remediation” refers to addressing a problem such as mold. “Restoration” means work to help return affected spaces to usable condition.
