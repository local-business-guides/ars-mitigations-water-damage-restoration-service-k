Scope, timing, and terms to know

The exact work and schedule depend on the property and the size and condition of the area. Ask what needs to happen first, what decisions you will need to make, and how changes to the scope will be handled. There is no single timeline or cost that applies to every remodel. In general, the amount of repair, materials selected, access to the work area, and any damage found during the project can affect both.

Key facts to discuss:
- What it covers: the rooms or features included, plus any repair work connected to the project.
- Typical timing: ask for a project-specific schedule; the scope and property conditions shape it.
- Cost factors: materials, labor, repair needs, and changes to the agreed work can all matter.

You may hear scope, meaning the work included in the project, and change order, meaning an agreed adjustment to that work. Ask for unfamiliar terms to be explained plainly.
