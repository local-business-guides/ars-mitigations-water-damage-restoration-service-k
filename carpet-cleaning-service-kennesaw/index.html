Carpet Cleaning Service Kennesaw: common questions

Can every carpet stain be removed?
Not necessarily. The material, the substance, and how long it has been there can all affect the result, so ask what outcome is realistic for your specific spot.
What should I ask before booking carpet cleaning?
Confirm which rooms or areas are included, what preparation is expected, and how the recommended method suits your carpet. Ask how timing and cost will be determined for the agreed scope.
When can I use the carpet again?
That depends on the work performed and the carpet. Ask the provider for guidance on drying time and when normal foot traffic can resume.
